How this works

Risk per share = absolute difference between entry and stop.

Shares = smaller of what your risk budget permits and what your available cash can purchase, rounded down to whole shares.

Settings & backups

Your data stays in this browser

Positions, watchlist, and journal are stored in local browser storage on this device. They do not automatically sync to other devices. Private browsing, clearing website data, or switching browsers can erase them.

Security

Never paste Alpaca credentials, account numbers, passwords, or private keys into a public GitHub repository or this static site. Anyone can access the published site. The site does not have user authentication.
